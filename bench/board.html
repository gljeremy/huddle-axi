Risks

risk

SSO users skip onboarding entirely

About a third of seats arrive by SSO and never see onboarding at all, so a redesign of the self-serve flow misses them.

severity highlinked to c7
risk

Data access is the real bottleneck

Most of the 23 minutes is people waiting for credentials from their own IT team. A faster wizard won't fix that.

  • Offer demo data so they can explore while they wait
  • Send a ready-made request email they can forward to IT
  • Let an admin pre-approve data sources for the whole workspace
severity highraised by Support
risk

Demo data gets mistaken for real data

If sample numbers look too real, someone will screenshot them into a deck.

severity mediumlinked to c6
risk

Q3 is already full, until mid-August

Two engineers are committed to the billing migration until mid-August. If one frees up in July, the wizard can still ship behind a flag before the August rollout.

  • Fallback: ship the SSO personal tour first, since it's smaller
severity highraised by Productlinked to c16

Ideas we haven't sorted yet

idea

Checklist that follows you

A small progress widget that stays in the corner for the first week, not just the first session.

idea

Invite a teammate as a setup step

Teams with two or more active people in week one retain much better. Make inviting someone part of setup.

idea

Ask the role, not the company size

Role predicts which dashboard people want far better than headcount does.

idea

Live chat only in the first hour

Support has capacity for a narrow window. Offer chat during the first hour after signup, when it matters most.
